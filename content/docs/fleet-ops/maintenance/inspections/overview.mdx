# Inspections

**Inspections** let you check vehicles against a standard checklist and keep a record of every result. Use them for daily walk-rounds, pre-trip and post-trip checks, safety and compliance audits, or damage assessments.

A consistent inspection record helps you:

- **Stay compliant** — every inspection is stored with who filed it, when, the answers given, and any photos or signatures.
- **Keep vehicles safe** — a failed check can be marked **Unsafe to operate**, so problems are flagged before the next trip.
- **Catch defects early** — failed checks become issues and work orders with one click, so nothing is noted and then forgotten.

You'll find inspections under **Fleet-Ops → Maintenance**, in two menu items: **Inspection Forms** and **Inspections**.

## Key Terms

| Term | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Inspection Form** | The checklist you design: field groups (such as *Exterior* or *Brakes*) holding the fields to answer. A form is **Draft**, **Published**, or **Archived**. |
| **Inspection** | One completed run of a form, recorded against a vehicle and driver. Its details page is titled after the form, for example *Daily Walk-round Submission*. |
| **Inspection Link** | A single-use link, protected by a 6-digit PIN, that lets someone fill in a published form without signing in to the console. |
| **Follow-up** | The issue or work order raised from an inspection's failed checks, and resolving the inspection once the follow-up is under way. |

## How Inspections Work

**Build a form** → **Carry out the inspection** (in the console, or through an inspection link) → **Submit** → **Review the result** → **Follow up failed checks** → **Track the fix on the issue or work order**

1. **Build a form.** Group the things to check and add a field for each one. **Pass / Fail** fields decide whether an inspection passes. Publish the form when it's ready.
2. **Carry out the inspection.** Fill it in yourself from **Maintenance → Inspections**, or generate an inspection link and send it, with its PIN, to the person doing the check.
3. **Submit.** Filing the inspection records the time and counts its passed and failed checks.
4. **Review the result.** Every inspection shows a **Result** of **Passed** or **Failed**, how many checks failed, the answers, photos, and who submitted it.
5. **Follow up.** Create an issue or a work order from the failed checks. Forms can also raise an issue or work order automatically when an inspection link submission has failures.
6. **Track the fix.** The issue and work order link back to the inspection, and the inspection links to them. Resolve the inspection once follow-up is in hand.

## Where Inspections Appear

- **Maintenance → Inspections** — every inspection, with its result and failed count.
- **Inspection Forms → [Form] → Submissions** — every inspection filed against that form.
- **Vehicles → [Vehicle] → Inspections** — the vehicle's inspection history.
- **Issues** and **Work Orders** — a **Linked Inspection** section on any issue or work order raised from an inspection.
- **Maintenance Hub** — a **Failed Inspections** count that takes you to the inspections list.
- **Radar** — failed inspections that have no issue or work order yet, follow-up still open, drafts that were never filed, and inspection links about to expire unused.

## Permissions

Inspection forms and inspections have their own permissions, which you grant through roles and policies in [Identity & Access](/docs/platform/identity-and-access/roles-and-permissions). Besides the usual view, create, update, and delete permissions, forms have separate **publish** and **archive** permissions, and inspections have separate **submit**, **create issue**, **create work order**, and **resolve** permissions. Actions you don't have permission for are hidden in the console.

The built-in **MaintenanceManager** and **OperationsAdmin** policies include full access to inspection forms and inspections.
